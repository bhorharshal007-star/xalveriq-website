    FAQ

    Questions owners ask us

    Do I need to change how my team works?

    No. Your team keeps chatting on WhatsApp as usual. The system handles the first reply, assignment, reminders and reports in the background, and we train your staff in a one-hour session.

    Who owns the WhatsApp number and data?

    You do. The WhatsApp number, provider account, Google Sheets or CRM, domain and code are all set up in your name. If you stop working with us, everything keeps running.

    How long does setup take?

    Most systems go live in 7 to 10 days after the scope is agreed. Websites usually take a similar time depending on content.

    What is included in the starting price, and what is extra?

    The starting price covers the standard workflow for your industry. Extra workflows, AI reply assistants, custom integrations and maintenance are quoted separately before we begin. WhatsApp message fees are paid directly to the provider.

    Is patient and client data safe?

    We follow consent-based messaging and collect only what is needed. For clinics we automate logistics like bookings and reminders, and do not store medical reports or diagnoses in our systems.

    How do payments work?

    50% to start and 50% on go-live. Every project includes 14 days of free fixes after launch.
