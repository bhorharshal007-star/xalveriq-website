# Xalveriq: services, pricing and website prompt

## 1. Services (copy for the Services section)

**Xalveriq** builds done-for-you business automation: WhatsApp + CRM + AI follow-up systems and booking-ready websites. Leads from Meta ads, Google ads, Instagram, the website, missed calls, walk-ins and property portals are captured in one place. Each lead gets a WhatsApp reply in under 60 seconds, goes to the right person, and is followed up until it books. Fixed scope, fixed price, live in about 10 days. The client owns every account.

| Service | For | What it does | Starting price |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Admission Engine** | Coaching centres and training institutes | Instant WhatsApp reply with brochure and batch details, counsellor assignment, follow-ups for 10 days, demo class booking and reminders, daily 8 pm owner report | ₹7,000 |
| **Patient Desk** | Clinics and diagnostic centres | Missed-call to WhatsApp auto-reply, booking link, confirmations and reminders, home collection and report-ready alerts, review requests, 30/90-day recalls | ₹7,000 |
| **Patient Desk for Dentists** | Dental clinics | Appointment booking, reminders, treatment follow-ups, review requests, 6-month recalls | ₹7,000 |
| **Quick Follow-up** | Real estate agencies and developers | Portal and ad leads in one pipeline, instant brochure, site visit booking, agent routing, visit reminders, no-show follow-up | ₹9,000+ |
| **Practice Automation** | CA firms and accountants | Document collection checklists on WhatsApp, GST/ITR/TDS deadline reminders, client onboarding, status updates | ₹7,000 |
| **Candidate Pipeline** | Recruitment, staffing and resume collection agencies | Resume intake from WhatsApp/forms/email, candidate tracker, shortlist alerts, interview scheduling, client updates | ₹9,000+ |
| **Custom flows** | Other high-value agencies (marketing, travel, education, direct agencies) | Custom lead capture and follow-up for high-ticket leads | ₹9,000+ |
| **Website and Booking** | Any local business (salons, gyms, clinics) | Fast, mobile-first website with services, reviews, maps, WhatsApp button, booking form, local SEO | ₹7,000 |

**Charged extra:** additional workflows, AI reply assistant, custom CRM or integrations, monthly maintenance, and WhatsApp or provider usage fees (the client pays these directly to the provider).

**Payment terms:** 50% to start, 50% on go-live. 14 days of free fixes after launch.

## 2. Prompt to give a website builder (or an AI) for the Services section

> Build the Services section for **Xalveriq**, a technical business automation agency in India. Tone: clear, confident, technical but plain-English, no hype, no emojis, no em-dashes. Brand colours: ink #0f0e0b, paper #f7f4ec, teal #12b3a4, mustard #ffc531, coral #ff5b57, sky #3aa0ff. Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque (headings), DM Sans (body), JetBrains Mono (labels).
>
> Section heading: "Automation systems built for how your business actually gets clients." Sub-line: "Fixed scope, fixed price, done for you. We set it up, train your team and hand over every account."
>
> Show six service cards, each with an icon, a small mono label for who it is for, a title, a one-line promise, three bullet points and a "from ₹X" price tag:
> 1. Admission Engine · Coaching centres and training institutes · from ₹7,000
> 2. Patient Desk · Clinics, diagnostics, dental and local businesses · from ₹7,000
> 3. Quick Follow-up · Real estate agencies and developers · from ₹9,000
> 4. Practice Automation · CA firms and accountants · from ₹7,000
> 5. Candidate Pipeline · Recruitment, staffing and high-value agencies · from ₹9,000
> 6. Website and Booking · Any local business · from ₹7,000
>
> Below the cards, add a dark band titled "One system for every lead source" listing: Meta ads, Google ads, Instagram, Website, WhatsApp, Missed calls, Walk-ins, Property portals.
>
> Animation: cards fade and slide up one after another on scroll; on hover each card lifts 6px, shows a coloured top bar and the icon tilts slightly. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Mobile: one column.
